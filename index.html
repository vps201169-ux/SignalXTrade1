    INDICATOR LAB

    Decode the market

    Learn what popular indicators measure. These are educational explanations, not current trading signals.

    RSI

    Relative Strength Index measures the speed and magnitude of recent price changes. Traders often study readings above 70 or below 30, but these are not automatic sell or buy signals.

    MACD

    Moving Average Convergence Divergence compares moving averages to study momentum and potential trend changes.

    EMA

    Exponential Moving Average gives more weight to recent prices. Traders use different EMA periods to study trends.
